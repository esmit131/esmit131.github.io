I'm a Computer Information Systems major at ASU. This site is where I show my personal projects I have done, what they did, and what I learned from doing each one.

What's on this site?

I have my own dedicated hardware for setting up virtual machines, self-hosted cloud services, reverse proxies, and more. Each project page has pictures, an explanation of why I did it, what it does, and what I learned. This site is the result of my final project for my CIS 300 course at ASU. The purpose of this is to demonstrate the HTML and CSS skills I've learned from this class. A brief list of some requirements for this site:

  • 1 Home page
  • 3-5 Content pages
  • 1 Table having at least 3 columns, 1 header row, and 3 data rows
  • 1 Video (specifically using the <video> tag)
  • 1 Email link
  • 1 External link
  • 1 Form with at least 4 fields
